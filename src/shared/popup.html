How it works & privacy

Sites can expire or revoke logins. Switching does not hide your identity or prevent bans. Other Chrome login storage remains shared.

Everything stays on your device. Chrome saves sensitive cookies without additional encryption. Firefox keeps login data in native containers. Removing an account deletes its saved entry, not the website account or Firefox container. To log out, use the website, then remove its saved entry from Switchboard.
