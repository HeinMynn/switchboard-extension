How it works & privacy

Sites can expire or revoke logins. Switching does not hide your identity or prevent bans. Other Chrome login storage remains shared.

Everything stays on your device. Chrome saves sensitive cookies without additional encryption. Firefox keeps login data in native containers. Forget removes a saved entry, not the website account or Firefox container. To log out, use the website, then forget its saved entry.
