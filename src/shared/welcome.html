GET STARTED

A little setup. A lot less switching around.

  1. 01

    Keep it within reach

    Open your browser’s Extensions menu and pin Switchboard to the toolbar. Click the green switch icon to open it.

  2. 02

    Save your first account

    Choose a website and click Connect. Sign in normally, then choose Add account → Save the login already open. Give it a name and click Save account.

  3. 03

    Make space for another

    Save your work and close the website’s tabs in all Chrome windows. Choose Add account → Create & open, sign in, then return to Switchboard and click Save account.

EVERYDAY USE

Back to the right account.

Close the website’s tabs, then choose Switch & open beside the account you want. Check the name shown on the website before using it. Click Save again after login changes and before quitting Chrome.

A few useful things to know

Make it yours

Choose Manage account → Rename to change an account’s label. Use the website picker to move between sites, or Add website to connect another.

Export a saved login

Choose Manage account → Export cookies, then .json or .txt. Both contain JSON from the saved snapshot. Save again first for current cookies. Keep exports private: they can grant account access.

Remove is not logout

Forget account deletes its saved snapshot. Remove website deletes all its saved entries. Current website cookies remain. To sign out, log out on the website, then forget the saved account.

Returning after a restart?

If the selected login needs restoring, close the site’s tabs and choose Manage account → Restore saved login. If a switch is interrupted, use Recover previous session.

About staying signed in

Websites can expire or revoke sessions. Chrome switches cookies; other login storage remains shared, so compatibility varies by website. Switchboard does not hide your identity or guarantee protection from bans.
